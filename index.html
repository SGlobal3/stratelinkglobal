What We Do

Stratelink Global is an international trade operator registered in Dubai under IFZA. We act on our own account or as a commission-based intermediary — connecting serious buyers with verified Chinese manufacturers, handling everything in between.

01
Supplier Sourcing & Verification
Factory audit, sample validation, compliance checks. We never pass unverified suppliers.
02
Price Negotiation & Contracting
Direct factory pricing, bilingual contracts EN/FR/ZH, commercial terms structuring.
03
Logistics & Customs Coordination
FCL / LCL, freight booking, B/L tracking, customs clearance support at destination.

Trade Finance Structuring
T/T, L/C, D/P — we advise on payment structure to protect both parties.

Dubai Hub Transit & Consolidation
Using Dubai as a re-export platform for multi-destination African shipments.
